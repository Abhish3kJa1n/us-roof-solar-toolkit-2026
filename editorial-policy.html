Editorial Policy

How this site prepares calculators and homeowner information, describes uncertainty and handles corrections.

Home / Editorial Policy

Scope and responsibility

Roof & Solar Toolkit is an independently maintained U.S.-focused planning resource. Its calculators and guides are intended for general educational use. They are not property inspections, professional designs, contractor quotations, legal or tax advice, or guarantees of project cost or performance.

The site does not represent that its calculators are professionally certified or independently reviewed unless a specific page identifies an actual reviewer and the scope of that review. No editor, expert credential or review relationship is implied by a generic site or team label.

Publisher identity and accountability

Roof & Solar Toolkit is independently operated by one publisher under the publication's name. That publisher is responsible for research, calculator maintenance, source documentation and corrections. Brand-based attribution identifies the publisher responsible for a page; it does not imply a separate editorial team, professional qualification or independent technical review.

Guides use the attribution “Prepared and maintained by Roof & Solar Toolkit” to identify the publication responsible for their content. A page-specific professional reviewer is named only when a real person has reviewed the stated material and agreed to be identified.

Source selection

When practical, primary sources are preferred: government datasets and publications, utility tariffs and documentation, technical model documentation, and original research. Secondary sources may be used for market context when primary data do not provide a directly comparable measure. A secondary estimate is identified as such and is not presented as an official government figure.

Sources should be relevant to the claim being made. Dates, units, geography, sample size and methodology matter. A national figure is not described as a city price; a state-average electricity rate is not described as a household tariff; historical project data are not described as a current quote. Where a source's license or commercial reuse rights are unclear, its data are not published as a calculator benchmark until those rights are resolved.

Calculator methods and limitations

Calculators use documented formulas, visitor inputs and explicitly identified reference values. The Calculation Methodology describes current formulas, examples, source references, assumptions and limitations. A formula example is illustrative unless the page says it is a measured or source-derived result. For the solar energy-value calculations, a blank required tariff field is treated as unknown rather than zero; other optional inputs follow the instructions shown in the individual calculator. The site does not claim property-level precision from representative city coordinates.

Technical ranges used for input validation are guardrails against implausible or mistyped entries; they are not claims that every value within a range is typical, recommended or physically suitable. Visitors should compare outputs with current local documentation and qualified professional advice.

Updates and dates

Material changes to formulas, source datasets, benchmark values or important limitations should be reflected in the relevant page or its linked methodology. A displayed revision date describes a page or method revision; it does not mean every linked source or dataset was freshly rechecked on that date. Source publication dates and data periods should be distinguished from the date a page was edited. Dates are not advanced merely to make a page appear fresh.

Corrections and feedback

Readers can report a suspected factual error, broken source, misleading description or calculator defect by emailing getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com. Include the affected page URL, the statement or input involved, what appears incorrect and a reliable supporting source where possible. For a calculator issue, include the inputs and expected versus displayed result. Do not email home addresses, utility account numbers, unredacted bills, passwords or other sensitive personal information.

Email is a direct contact channel; messages are not published as public issue threads. The site does not promise a response time. Reports are assessed for reproducibility and source quality; confirmed material errors should be corrected in the relevant page, calculator or methodology, with the explanation updated when needed. A report is not automatically accepted merely because it is submitted. For general enquiries or privacy requests, use the Contact page.

Advertising and editorial independence

Advertising, if displayed, is separate from the site's calculation methods and editorial explanations. An advertisement or commercial relationship does not establish that a product, installer, utility or service is recommended or endorsed. Sponsored or paid content, if ever published, should be clearly identified.

Professional review

Roof measurements, structural capacity, roof condition, electrical design, utility interconnection, permitting, financing and tax eligibility can require qualified professionals or official determinations. The site does not substitute for those services. It will not invent credentials, imply a professional review that did not occur, or present a calculator as a certified assessment.
