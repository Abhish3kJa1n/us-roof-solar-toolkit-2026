Research and sources

When practical, primary sources are preferred: government datasets and publications, utility tariffs and documentation, technical model documentation, and original research. Secondary sources may be used for market context when primary data do not provide a directly comparable measure. Secondary estimates are identified as such and are not presented as official government figures.

Sources are assessed for relevance to the claim being made. Dates, units, geography, sample size and methodology matter. A national figure is not described as a city price; a state-average electricity rate is not described as a household tariff; historical project data are not described as a current quote. Data with unclear licensing or commercial reuse rights are not used as calculator benchmarks until those rights are resolved.

2026 source examples

Current reference examples include NLR PVWatts V8 for modeled solar production; U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb for representative place points; OpenEI's Utility Rate Database for nearby tariff candidates; EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.B for state and national residential rate context; and published 2026 EnergySage and Angi market ranges where clearly labelled as secondary national references. The source, date, geography and limitations are identified where a figure is used. A national average is not treated as a household tariff or local quote.

The federal Residential Clean Energy Credit under Section 25D is not available for expenditures after December 31, 2025 under current law. The IRS describes the installation-completion timing test and separate rules for construction or reconstruction. The site does not give individual tax advice or calculate tax eligibility.

Calculator methods and limitations

Calculators use documented formulas, visitor inputs and identified reference values. The Calculation Methodology describes current formulas, examples, source references and assumptions. Illustrative examples are not market benchmarks unless the page identifies them as source-derived results.

Representative city coordinates do not establish a property's exact roof location or utility service territory. Technical input ranges help catch implausible or mistyped entries; they do not mean every value within a range is typical, recommended or suitable for a particular property. Visitors should compare results with current local documents and qualified professional advice.

Accuracy and updates

Material changes to formulas, source datasets, benchmark values or important limitations are reflected in the relevant page or its linked methodology. Page revision dates identify when page content was materially reviewed or changed. They do not mean every linked source or dataset was freshly rechecked on that date. Source publication dates and data periods are distinguished from page revision dates.

Corrections and feedback

Readers can report a suspected factual error, broken source, misleading description or calculator defect by emailing editor@getroofsolarcalculator.online Include the affected page URL, the statement or calculator input, what appears incorrect and a reliable supporting source where possible. For a calculator issue, include the inputs and expected versus displayed result. Do not email passwords, utility account numbers, unredacted bills or other sensitive personal information.

Reports are assessed for reproducibility and source quality. Confirmed material errors are corrected in the relevant page, calculator or methodology, with an explanation where useful. Submitting a report does not guarantee a response or mean the report has been verified. For general enquiries or privacy requests, use the Contact page.

Advertising and editorial standards

Advertising is separate from calculation methods and editorial explanations. An advertisement or commercial relationship does not mean a product, installer, utility or service is recommended or endorsed. Sponsored or paid content, if published, is identified clearly.

Professional services

Roof condition, structural capacity, electrical design, utility interconnection, permitting, financing and tax eligibility may require qualified professionals or official determinations. The site's calculators and guides provide general planning information and do not replace those services. A professional reviewer is identified only when an actual review has taken place and its scope can be stated.
