Editorial Policy

How we prepare homeowner calculators and guides.

Roof & Solar Toolkit publishes practical tools and explainers for U.S. homeowners planning roof work and residential solar.

Sources

We link to relevant sources and prefer government, utility and original technical information where available. Published cost surveys are identified as source estimates, not as prices for an individual home.

Calculators

Calculators use visitor inputs and stated assumptions. Results are estimates and may differ from a property's actual condition, local price, utility tariff or system design.

Updates

Information can change. Check linked sources and local requirements before making a project decision.

Advertising

Advertising, if shown, will be identified.
