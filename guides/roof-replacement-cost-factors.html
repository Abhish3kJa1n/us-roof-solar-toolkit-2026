Before comparing the totals

Ask each contractor to quote the same roof area, roofing system and work scope. If the proposals describe different products or leave different items out, their totals are not directly comparable. Compare the written proposals against the rows below; this page does not collect or save information.

3. Separate the quoted price from possible extras

Do not silently treat an allowance as a fixed price or assume an excluded item is included. Record only amounts the contractor has put in writing. Ask for a revised proposal if a material part of the work is unclear.

This is a comparison worksheet, not a formula for predicting the final invoice. Do not add speculative costs for concealed damage. Get the unit rate, measurement method and homeowner approval process in writing before work begins.

4. Resolve these points before signing

  • Verify contractor licensing where required, insurance and any local registration directly with the relevant authority or insurer.
  • Ask who obtains permits and schedules inspections, and confirm local requirements independently.
  • Get product names, installation details, exclusions, cleanup and warranty terms in the signed contract or an attached scope sheet.
  • Understand the deposit, payment milestones, cancellation terms and how additional work is approved and priced.
  • Ask each bidder to explain any unusually low price or missing scope item in writing.

How to use the result

A bid with a lower total may still leave more work unpriced. First resolve missing scope and unclear allowances; then compare the proposals on the work that is actually specified. A contractor's inspection and written proposal—not this worksheet—must determine what the roof needs.

For national cost context and published price ranges, see the roof replacement cost guide. For a broad arithmetic scenario based on measured area and rates you enter, use the roof replacement cost calculator.

Reference

The National Roofing Contractors Association's consumer roofing resources provide guidance on selecting roofing systems and reviewing proposals. Local licensing, permit and insurance requirements vary; check the relevant local authority and insurer.
