Describe the solar array

Start with a 1 kW DC reference system, 20° tilt, south-facing azimuth and 14% losses. Adjust these values to match a proposal or your roof-plane scenario.

How to read the estimate

PVWatts models production for the selected city point and array settings.

Production is not bill savings

The value of solar electricity depends on when it is used, the utility's price for electricity you buy, any export credit for surplus power and other rate-plan rules (often called the tariff). This tool estimates production only. Use your own bill and the utility's current published rate plan for a savings calculation.
