Estimate panel count from electricity use

For a usage-based estimate, enter 12 months of electricity consumption, your own target annual generation offset, and annual AC production per kW DC from a location-specific PVWatts model or a comparable installer model.

Use annual kWh from your utility bills, not the total dollar amount paid.
Choose your own target from 1% to 100%. Annual generation offset is not the same as bill reduction.
Use the annual kWh per kW DC shown by the city solar production estimator or a comparable site-specific proposal. It depends on location and array assumptions.
Use the actual module rating, not a generic market average.
Use the module dimensions/specification if you want the combined module surface area.
Already have a proposed system size? Leave annual use, target offset and production blank, then enter the quoted DC system size below. Do not fill both methods at once.
Use this alternative when an installer proposal already specifies the array's DC nameplate capacity.
