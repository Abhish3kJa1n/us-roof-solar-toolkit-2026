Privacy Notice

This notice describes the current Roof & Solar Toolkit website. The site offers homeowner planning calculators and guides. It does not currently run display ads, analytics, account registration, quote requests or a site-hosted contact form.

Calculator information

The roof, roofing-cost, and basic savings calculations run in your browser. Their inputs are not sent to the site's calculator endpoints. The solar production and nearby utility-rate features work differently: they send the location and model inputs described below to this site and the named data providers.

City and state lookup

If you use the location search, the city or place name and state abbreviation are sent to a server endpoint hosted with Cloudflare and queried against the U.S. Census Bureau's TIGERweb service. The result is a Census place representative point, not your address, parcel, roof location, or utility service territory. Exact lookup responses are not cached by this site; search suggestions may be cached briefly.

Solar production estimate

If you request a solar estimate, the selected representative latitude and longitude, system capacity, tilt, azimuth and loss assumptions are sent through this site's Cloudflare-hosted endpoint to the National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR) PVWatts V8 service. A server-side API key and a short-lived signed location proof are used by the endpoint; neither is sent to your browser as an API key. The endpoint does not ask for your street address, utility account, bill, annual household usage or installer quote.

Nearby utility-rate search

If you request nearby rate records, the selected representative latitude and longitude are sent through this site's Cloudflare-hosted endpoint to the OpenEI Utility Rate Database. The returned records are candidates only. They do not confirm your utility, tariff, current rate, export credit or eligibility.

Request limits and technical logs

The site uses a Cloudflare D1 database to count aggregate API requests by service and time window so it can enforce shared usage limits. The quota records contain service names, time buckets, counters and update times; the application quota table does not store calculator inputs, place names or visitor IP addresses. Cloudflare may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser and request metadata, to deliver and protect the site under its own service terms and privacy practices. This site does not control Cloudflare's infrastructure logging or retention.

Advertising, cookies and analytics

The site is registered with Google AdSense for publisher verification and review. The homepage contains the publisher verification meta tag, and the root ads.txt file declares the publisher account. As of November 8, 2026, AdSense ad serving is disabled: the ad loader is off by default, no display ads are being requested by this site, and no analytics script is active.

If Google approves the site and the publisher later enables ad serving, the initial loader scope is limited to the editorial guide pages and the electricity-prices-by-state reference page. It is not loaded on the homepage, calculator pages, Privacy Notice or Terms page. No manual ad-unit slots or IDs are currently configured. The loader alone will not create manual ad placements. Actual placements would require owner-created manual ad units or separate Auto ads settings in the publisher account; the code does not itself switch on Auto ads or alter those dashboard settings.

When AdSense is active, the browser sends information to Google as part of the ad request. This can include the page URL and IP address. Google may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to deliver ads, measure advertising, prevent fraud and, depending on the publisher settings and applicable user choices, personalize ads. Third parties may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons and IP addresses as a result of ad serving. The site does not receive or control Google's advertising cookies or its infrastructure-level processing.

For more information about Google's handling of information on partner sites, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. When personalized advertising is used, you can manage Google ad personalization at Google Ads Settings and opt out of some participating third-party personalized advertising at aboutads.info choices. Availability and effect depend on your browser, region and the participating services. Your browser can also block or clear cookies, although this may affect advertising functionality.

The AdSense loader remains disabled until the site is approved and the publisher has reviewed applicable privacy, consent and opt-out requirements for the locations served. The Privacy Notice must be reviewed again before activation and whenever advertising providers, settings or placements change.

External links

This site links to government agencies, technical sources, Census, PVWatts, OpenEI and other providers. Their own privacy practices apply when you follow those links.

Your choices

You can choose not to use the location-based solar and rate features; the other browser-based calculators remain available.

Changes

This notice will be revised when the site's data flows or services change. The date above identifies the latest published revision.
