Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Roof & Solar Toolkit uses information when you visit getroofsolarcalculator.online, use its roof and solar calculators, request location-based estimates or contact the site. It describes the website's current practices, not the separate privacy practices of external providers.

1. Who operates this website

This website is presented and operated under the name Roof & Solar Toolkit. It provides free roof and residential-solar planning tools and information for U.S. homeowners. For privacy questions or requests, contact the site using getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com

2. Meaning of personal information

In this policy, personal information means information that identifies, relates to, describes or could reasonably be linked to a person or household. The precise legal definition and the rights attached to it depend on the privacy law that applies to you.

3. Information we collect

The information involved depends on which features you use. The site does not require an account, does not operate a quote-request form and does not ask for a street address, utility account credentials, payment details or an unredacted electricity bill.

Calculator entries

Roof-area, roof-replacement and standard solar-savings calculations are generally performed in your browser. The site does not ask you to submit those entries to a quote service. If you use a location-based feature, only the location and model inputs needed for the requested service are sent as described below.

The combined estimator can temporarily preserve selected entries in your browser while you open its city-based solar lookup and return to the calculation. This browser storage is limited to the current tab, expires after one hour and is removed after the form is restored or the tab is closed. It is not a website account or a server-side saved profile.

Location searches and calculator links

Page URL and query-string information

When you type a city search, the search text is sent to the site's location service, which queries U.S. Census Bureau place records for matching locations. When you select a city and state, those values are used to verify the place and obtain a representative Census point. The point represents a general Census place; it is not your street address, parcel, roof coordinates or confirmed utility service territory.

Some calculator links carry information in the page URL so another calculator can be prefilled. Depending on the link, the URL may include a selected city and state, modeled production figures or a source-based scenario value. The requested URL is processed by the site's hosting and delivery provider and may appear in technical request records. Do not put names, street addresses, account details or other sensitive information into a URL.

Automatically collected device and usage information

When you visit the site, Cloudflare and other enabled service providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device and operating-system details, requested page or service, timestamps, referrer and other request metadata. This information helps deliver pages, maintain security, troubleshoot failures and understand general site performance.

Information you send us

Messages and contact information

If you email the site, the email address used to send the message and its contents are received so the enquiry can be handled. The site does not have a general website contact form. Do not send passwords, utility account numbers, unredacted bills or other sensitive personal or financial information.

4. How information is collected

Information may be obtained in three ways:

  • Directly from you: when you enter calculator assumptions, search for a city or email the site.
  • Automatically: through website hosting, security and analytics services when your browser requests pages or uses site features.
  • From service responses: when a requested feature receives Census place information, a PVWatts production model or nearby OpenEI utility-rate records.

5. How we use information

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • Run the calculator or location-based feature you choose and return its result.
  • Identify a general Census place for a requested solar or utility-rate lookup.
  • Host, deliver, secure, maintain and troubleshoot the website and its services.
  • Measure general website usage and performance and identify problems with pages or features.
  • Respond to questions, correction reports and privacy requests.
  • Comply with applicable law and protect the website, its users and the rights or safety of others.

The site does not use calculator results to provide contractor quotes, sell household leads or make decisions about a person's eligibility for a financial product.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not set cookies for analytics, does not use localStorage or sessionStorage for analytics identifiers, and does not fingerprint individual visitors. It measures aggregate page-view and performance information such as page paths, referrers, browser and operating-system family, country and Core Web Vitals metrics.

The combined calculator uses browser session storage for a separate functional purpose: temporarily preserving selected form entries in the current tab while you open the city-based solar lookup and return. That temporary calculator state is not an analytics identifier, expires after one hour and is removed after restoration or when the tab is closed.

Cloudflare may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms to deliver and protect the site. These are separate from Cloudflare Web Analytics. You can manage browser storage and cookies through your browser settings; blocking required site functionality may prevent some features from working.

8. Advertising, sale and sharing

No advertising is currently displayed on this site, and the current production configuration does not enable the AdSense ad-serving script. If advertising is introduced, this policy and any required notices or preference mechanisms will be reviewed and updated before advertising is enabled.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do disclose limited information to Cloudflare to host, deliver and secure the site, and to the Census Bureau, NLR or OpenEI when you request the location or modeling features described in section 6. Those operational disclosures are not used by this site to sell household leads or target advertising. Privacy-law definitions and obligations vary by jurisdiction, and this statement does not replace the rights or exceptions that may apply under a particular law.

9. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is used, subject to applicable legal, security and recordkeeping requirements. The retention approach differs by information type:

  • Calculator entries: ordinary entries remain in the active browser page. The combined estimator's temporary browser save expires after one hour and is removed after the form is restored or the tab is closed.
  • Location and model requests: the site's service processes the location and model settings needed to return the requested result. Census, NLR and OpenEI handle requests under their own service terms and policies.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: Cloudflare's dashboard provides access to the preceding six months of Web Analytics reports. This is the reporting window shown in the dashboard, not a claim about the retention period for raw beacon events.
  • Website delivery and security records: Cloudflare applies its own service, security and account retention practices to technical request information.
  • Email correspondence: messages are kept for as long as reasonably needed to answer the enquiry, handle related follow-up, maintain relevant records and address legal obligations or disputes.

When information is no longer reasonably needed, it should be deleted or de-identified where appropriate, subject to applicable legal requirements and provider retention practices.

10. Data security

The site uses HTTPS and security features provided by its hosting and delivery services to help protect information in transit and to operate its online features. Access to third-party services and their systems is also subject to those providers' security controls. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and the site cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International processing and data transfers

Cloudflare, Google and other service providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they or their service providers operate. Privacy protections and legal rules may differ between locations. The relevant provider's privacy and transfer terms apply to information it processes.

12. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on your state of residence, the type of information involved and whether the applicable law covers the site operator, you may have rights to request access to or a copy of personal information, request correction or deletion, obtain information about collection and disclosure, receive portable information, or opt out of certain sale, sharing or targeted-advertising processing. Some laws also provide rights to limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, appeal a denied request or exercise rights without discrimination.

To submit a privacy request, send an email to getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com and include the site you are contacting, your state of residence, the right you wish to exercise and enough detail for us to understand the request. Do not include passwords, utility account credentials or unredacted bills. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request or an authorized agent's authority. Requests will be handled under the law that applies to the request and the site operator.

Not every privacy law applies to every website operator, and rights, exceptions and response periods differ. This policy does not state that the site is covered by every U.S. state privacy law or that every listed right is available in every state.

13. Children's privacy

This site is intended for homeowners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the site, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

14. Links to other websites

The site links to government agencies, data providers and other websites for reference. When you follow an external link, the destination site's privacy policy and practices apply. We do not control the content or privacy practices of external websites.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the site's information practices, service providers or legal obligations materially change. The effective date at the top of the page identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected in the updated policy.
