Privacy Notice

This notice explains how information is handled when you use Roof & Solar Toolkit, a U.S. homeowner resource for roof and solar planning. By using the site, you understand that the services described below may process the information needed to operate the features you choose.

Calculator information

Most calculator entries are processed in your browser. Calculator inputs are not sent to the site's calculation APIs for the standard roof and solar calculations.

If you use the combined estimator's city-based solar lookup, information entered in the estimator may be temporarily retained in the current browser tab so you can return to your calculation. It is cleared after it is restored or when the tab is closed. Do not enter information you do not want stored in your browser.

Location and third-party data services

Some features use a city or place selection to retrieve public reference information. The location search checks the selected place and state against the U.S. Census Bureau's TIGERweb service. It uses a representative Census location, not your street address or the precise position of your home.

When you request a solar production estimate, the representative location and system assumptions you provide are sent to the National Laboratory of the Rockies PVWatts V8 service to calculate modeled production. The feature does not ask for your street address.

When you request nearby utility-rate information, the representative location is used to search the OpenEI Utility Rate Database. Returned rates are possible matches, not confirmation of your utility, tariff or export-credit terms.

Hosting and website analytics

Cloudflare provides website hosting, delivery and security services. As part of operating and protecting the site, Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request information under its own privacy practices.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is used for aggregate website performance measurement, including browser performance timing metrics. Cloudflare states that this service does not collect or use visitors' personal data, or track individual end users across customers' internet properties. See Cloudflare Web Analytics for details.

Google Analytics website measurement is also used to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as user and session statistics, approximate geolocation, and browser and device information. It may use a first-party cookie named _ga to distinguish browsers and sessions, depending on the Analytics settings and browser controls. Google explains its Analytics data collection and data safeguards.

The site does not intentionally attach calculator values, roof measurements, electricity-bill amounts, utility-rate inputs, street addresses, names or contact details to Google Analytics events. Google Analytics is used for site measurement. This site does not currently display an analytics consent banner or use Google Consent Mode. Analytics behavior may also depend on your browser settings and applicable regional requirements.

Advertising

This site is not currently displaying advertising or loading an AdSense ad-serving script. If advertising is introduced, this notice will be updated to describe the advertising services in use, the information they process and the choices available to visitors.

External websites

Links to government agencies, data providers and other websites are provided for reference. Their own privacy notices and practices apply when you visit those services.

Your choices and privacy enquiries

You can choose not to use location-based features. You can also use your browser settings to manage cookies and site data or block requests. Some features may not work if required requests are blocked.

For a privacy question or request, contact getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com. Please include only the information needed to handle your request. Do not email passwords, utility account credentials or unredacted bills.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated when the site's information practices or services materially change. The revision date appears at the top of this page.
