Privacy Notice

Roof & Solar Toolkit respects your privacy. This notice explains what information is used when you visit the site and how it supports the calculators and other features.

Information used by the calculators

Most calculator entries, including roof measurements, electricity use and cost assumptions, are processed in your browser. They are not sent to our calculation services.

If you use the combined estimator's city-based solar lookup, your entries are temporarily saved in your browser so you can return to the calculation. They are removed after they are restored or when you close the tab. This temporary save expires after one hour.

When you search for a city, the city or place name and state are used to find a matching U.S. Census place. The selected place represents a general location, not your home address. If you request a solar production estimate, that location and the system settings you enter are sent to the National Laboratory of the Rockies PVWatts service. If you search for nearby utility rates, the location is sent to the OpenEI Utility Rate Database. These services return estimates or possible rate matches; they do not identify your exact property, utility account or applicable tariff.

Website operations and analytics

Cloudflare provides website hosting, delivery and security. In providing those services, it may process information such as your IP address, browser and device details, and information about requests to the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics is also used to measure website performance, including page-loading and browser performance metrics. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and its Web Analytics information.

Google Analytics is used to understand how visitors use the site. It may collect usage and session information, approximate location, and browser and device details. Depending on its settings, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including a cookie named _ga, to distinguish browsers and sessions. The site does not intentionally include calculator values, roof measurements, electricity-bill amounts, street addresses, names or contact details in Google Analytics events. Read Google's explanation of Analytics data collection and its Privacy Policy.

Hosting and analytics providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their retention periods and controls may depend on their service and settings.

Cookies and your choices

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. You can also choose not to use the location-based features. Blocking cookies or required site requests may affect some features. For information about Google's available controls, visit Google Analytics' browser opt-out.

Advertising

Roof & Solar Toolkit is not currently displaying advertisements. If advertising is added, this notice will be updated to identify the advertising services in use and explain relevant information practices and visitor choices.

Privacy rights and contact

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a privacy request, email getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com. Include your state or country of residence and enough detail for us to understand your request. Do not send passwords, utility account credentials or unredacted bills.

Other websites

This site links to government agencies, data providers and other websites. Their privacy policies apply when you visit those sites.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for homeowners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated when our information practices materially change. The revision date at the top of the page shows when this notice was last updated.
