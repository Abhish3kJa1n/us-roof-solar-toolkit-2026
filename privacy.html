Privacy Notice

This notice describes the current Roof & Solar Toolkit website. The site offers homeowner planning calculators and guides. It does not currently run display ads, analytics, account registration, quote requests or a site-hosted contact form.

Calculator information

The roof, roofing-cost, and basic savings calculations run in your browser. Their inputs are not sent to the site's calculator endpoints. The solar production and nearby utility-rate features work differently: they send the location and model inputs described below to this site and the named data providers.

City and state lookup

If you use the location search, the city or place name and state abbreviation are sent to a server endpoint hosted with Cloudflare and queried against the U.S. Census Bureau's TIGERweb service. The result is a Census place representative point, not your address, parcel, roof location, or utility service territory. Exact lookup responses are not cached by this site; search suggestions may be cached briefly.

Solar production estimate

If you request a solar estimate, the selected representative latitude and longitude, system capacity, tilt, azimuth and loss assumptions are sent through this site's Cloudflare-hosted endpoint to the National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR) PVWatts V8 service. A server-side API key and a short-lived signed location proof are used by the endpoint; neither is sent to your browser as an API key. The endpoint does not ask for your street address, utility account, bill, annual household usage or installer quote.

Nearby utility-rate search

If you request nearby rate records, the selected representative latitude and longitude are sent through this site's Cloudflare-hosted endpoint to the OpenEI Utility Rate Database. The returned records are candidates only. They do not confirm your utility, tariff, current rate, export credit or eligibility.

Request limits and technical logs

The site uses a Cloudflare D1 database to count aggregate API requests by service and time window so it can enforce shared usage limits. The quota records contain service names, time buckets, counters and update times; the application quota table does not store calculator inputs, place names or visitor IP addresses. Cloudflare may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser and request metadata, to deliver and protect the site under its own service terms and privacy practices. This site does not control Cloudflare's infrastructure logging or retention.

Advertising, cookies and analytics

This site is registered with Google AdSense for publisher site verification and review. The homepage contains the AdSense publisher verification meta tag, and the root ads.txt file identifies the publisher account. As of November 8, 2026, no AdSense ad-serving script, display ads or analytics script is active on this site. The guarded loader is staged behind separate build-time and runtime switches; neither switch is enabled by this code change. The verification meta tag and ads.txt record do not themselves request or display ads.

If Google approves the site and the publisher later activates AdSense after completing the required privacy, consent and opt-out review, the initial code scope is limited to editorial guide pages and the electricity-prices-by-state reference page. The homepage, calculator pages, Privacy Notice and Terms page are excluded so ad requests stay away from the calculator workflow. The loader does not create manual ad slots; any manual placement requires an owner-created ad unit and actual slot ID. Auto ads require a separate setting in the AdSense account and are not enabled by this code.

When ad serving is activated, the tag will cause the visitor's browser to make requests to Google and its advertising partners. Those requests can include the URL of the page being viewed, the visitor's IP address, browser/device information and information associated with cookies or other identifiers. Google may use this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and personalize ads where permitted by the visitor's settings and applicable requirements.

Google and other third-party advertising vendors may place and read cookies on the visitor's browser, use web beacons or similar technologies, and process IP addresses in connection with ad serving. Loading an advertising tag can cause requests to advertising systems even when a particular page view does not result in a visible ad. For that reason, the AdSense ad tag is not active now and will not be enabled until the publisher has completed the required privacy, consent and opt-out review for the locations served.

For details about Google's data practices on partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. When personalized Google advertising is used, visitors can manage their Google ad-personalization settings at Google Ads Settings. Visitors may also be able to opt out of some participating third-party personalized advertising through aboutads.info choices. These controls do not necessarily cover every advertising provider or every browser/device. Visitors can also block or clear cookies through browser settings, although doing so may affect advertising functionality.

No analytics provider is currently connected. If the site's advertising, analytics or consent-management services change, this notice will be reviewed and updated to describe the services actually used before they are activated.

External links

This site links to government agencies, technical sources, Census, PVWatts, OpenEI and other providers. Their own privacy practices apply when you follow those links.

Your choices

You can choose not to use the location-based solar and rate features; the other browser-based calculators remain available.

Changes

This notice will be revised when the site's data flows or services change. The date above identifies the latest published revision.
