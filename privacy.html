Privacy Policy

Roof & Solar Toolkit provides roof and solar planning tools for U.S. homeowners. This policy explains what information is used when you visit the site, use its calculators or contact us.

Calculator and location information

Most calculator entries, including roof measurements, electricity use and cost assumptions, are processed in your browser. They are not sent to our calculation services.

If you use the combined estimator's city-based solar lookup, your entries are temporarily saved in your browser so you can return to the calculation. This temporary save expires after one hour and is removed after restoration or when you close the tab.

When you search for a city, the city or place name and state are sent to the U.S. Census Bureau to identify a matching place. The selected place is a general location, not your home address. If you request a solar production estimate, that location and the system settings you enter are sent to the National Laboratory of the Rockies PVWatts service. If you search for nearby utility rates, the location is sent to the OpenEI Utility Rate Database. These services provide modeled production or possible rate matches; they do not identify your exact property, utility account or applicable tariff.

Website and device information

Cloudflare provides website hosting, delivery and security. It may process your IP address, browser and device details, and request information to provide and protect the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate website performance.

Google Analytics is used to understand general website visits and usage. It may collect usage and session information, approximate location, browser and device details, and cookie identifiers. Depending on its settings, it may use first-party cookies, including a cookie named _ga, to distinguish browsers and sessions. The site does not intentionally include calculator values, roof measurements, electricity-bill amounts, street addresses, names or contact details in Google Analytics events.

Cloudflare and Google process information under their own terms and privacy policies. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, operate and protect the website, understand general site usage, and respond to messages.

Contact messages and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information in your message. We use it to respond and keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any related follow-up. Do not send passwords, utility account credentials or unredacted bills.

The temporary calculator save described above lasts up to one hour. Hosting, security and analytics providers apply their own retention settings and policies to information they process.

Cookies and your choices

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. You can also choose not to use the location-based features. Blocking cookies or required service requests may affect some features. For Google's available controls, see Google Analytics' browser opt-out.

Advertising

Advertising is not currently displayed on this site.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and, where applicable, opt out of its sale, sharing or use for targeted advertising. To make a privacy request, email getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com. Include your state or country of residence and enough detail for us to understand your request.

Other websites and children's privacy

Links to government agencies, data providers and other websites are provided for reference. Their privacy policies apply when you visit those sites.

This site is intended for homeowners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when our information practices materially change. The revision date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
