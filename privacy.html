Privacy Policy

Roof & Solar Toolkit provides roof and solar planning tools for U.S. homeowners. This policy explains what information is handled when you visit the site, use its calculators or contact us.

Information we collect

Calculator entries. Standard roof and solar calculations run in your browser. If you use a location-based feature, the city or state you select and the system details needed for that estimate are sent to the relevant data services. The tools do not request your street address.

Website and device information. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process your IP address, browser information and request data to deliver and protect the site. Google Analytics is used to measure website visits and general usage. It may collect browser and device information, approximate location and cookie or similar identifiers. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate website performance.

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, utility account credentials or unredacted bills.

How information is used

Information is used to provide requested calculator features, operate and secure the website, understand general site usage, and respond to messages. Calculator entries are not used to create visitor accounts or quote requests.

Service providers and external links

Location-based estimates may use services from the U.S. Census Bureau, the National Laboratory of the Rockies' PVWatts service and the OpenEI Utility Rate Database. Cloudflare provides hosting, delivery and security. Google Analytics provides website measurement. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. When you follow a link to another website, that site's privacy policy applies.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including a cookie named _ga, to measure visits and distinguish browsers. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies or required service requests may affect some site features. You can also choose not to use the location-based calculators.

Storage and retention

If you use the combined estimator and move to its city-based solar lookup, a temporary copy of your calculator entries is stored in your browser for up to one hour so the calculation can be restored when you return. It is removed after restoration or when the browser tab is closed. Hosting, security and analytics providers apply their own retention settings and policies to the information they process.

Advertising

Advertising is not currently displayed on this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated to identify the advertising services in use and explain the information they collect and the choices available to visitors.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a privacy request, email getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com. Please describe your request and include only the information needed for us to respond. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when our information practices change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Questions about this policy can be sent to getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com.
