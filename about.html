About Roof & Solar Toolkit

Free planning calculators and practical information for U.S. homeowners considering roofing and residential solar projects.

Roof & Solar Toolkit is the name of this homeowner information website. It brings together calculators, explainers and source-linked references to help people understand project questions and prepare to compare options.

The tools use visitor-provided inputs and stated assumptions. Some solar features can model typical-year electricity production or find nearby utility-rate records; neither identifies a home's actual utility tariff. Roof and solar results are planning estimates, not property inspections, contractor quotes, engineering assessments, financial forecasts or professional advice.

How the site is supported

The site is free to use. Display advertising is not active in the current build; if it is added later, it will be identified as advertising. Advertising is not a recommendation or endorsement. No display ads or advertiser endorsements are active in the current build.

Sources and corrections

Guides and tools identify important sources, inputs, assumptions and limitations where applicable. Government and original technical sources are preferred for rules and technical data; commercial cost surveys are identified as the publishers' estimates, not measurements of a visitor's project. Content is not represented as independently reviewed by a licensed or certified professional. Readers can report corrections and calculator issues through the public project issue tracker; response times are not guaranteed. Do not post private information there.

This website does not offer roofing or solar installation, collect quote requests, sell homeowner leads, or act as a utility, government agency, lender or contractor marketplace.
