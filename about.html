About Roof & Solar Toolkit

A roof and solar homeowner information project. Complete publisher details before launch.

Roof & Solar Toolkit is a working name for a US-focused homeowner information and calculator website. Its goal is to provide useful tools and clear explanations around roofing and residential solar. It is not a contractor marketplace and does not collect quote leads through this demo.
