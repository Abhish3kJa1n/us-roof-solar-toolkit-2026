Contact Roof & Solar Toolkit

Send questions, report a calculator issue or suggest a correction.

Home / Contact

Email

For general questions, calculator feedback, source corrections or privacy-related requests, email getroofsolarcalculator@gmail.com.

For a correction, include the page URL, the relevant statement or calculator input, what appears incorrect and a reliable supporting source where possible. For a calculator issue, include the inputs and expected versus displayed result.

Protect your information

This site provides planning calculators; it does not need your street address, utility account number, passwords, payment information or an unredacted utility bill to receive ordinary feedback. Do not email sensitive personal or financial information. If an example is necessary to explain a calculator issue, use fictional or appropriately redacted values.

What to expect

Messages are reviewed as time permits. A reply or correction is not guaranteed, and submitting a report does not mean the report has been verified. Confirmed material errors may be corrected in the relevant page, calculator or methodology.

For details on how corrections are assessed, see the Editorial Policy. For the site's data practices, see the Privacy Notice.
