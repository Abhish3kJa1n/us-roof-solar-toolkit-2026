Corrections and site issues

Contact Roof & Solar Toolkit

The site does not collect contact details through a form. For a calculator bug, accessibility issue or content correction, use the project's public issue tracker:

Open the public issue tracker

What to include

Share the page URL, the relevant wording or calculator step, what you expected to happen, and a reliable source if the issue concerns a factual claim. Please do not post a home address, utility bill, account number, payment information or other private information. Issues are public and response times are not guaranteed.

For details about the information processed by the site and by GitHub when you submit an issue, read the Privacy Notice.
